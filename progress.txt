## Codebase Patterns
- **CMS Driven**: All quiz content (questions, answers, results) is fetched from Supabase.
- **State Management**: Uses a client-side store (likely Zustand or React Context) to track weights across 3 axes.
- **Stateless/Anonymous**: No user authentication. State is ephemeral or re-hydrated via payment tokens.
- **Styling**: "Clean & Clinical" aesthetic. Minimalist, Serif headers, Sans-serif body.
